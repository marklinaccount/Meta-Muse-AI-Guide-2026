<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. What Is Muse AI?</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Muse is Meta’s personal AI agent. It can plan tasks around your goals and carry them out using a browser and connected apps—for example, organizing email, looking up information, managing your calendar, and planning trips. For tasks that take time, Muse can keep working in the background and notify you when your input or approval is needed.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For global marketing and cross-border business teams, Muse can also help analyze campaign performance, organize customer communications, draft campaign materials, and work with business tools such as Facebook and Instagram. Available capabilities depend on your account and the connectors you have authorized.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As of October 27, 2026, Muse is available in the United States, Canada, and Japan. The service launched in Japan on October 23.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>However, some users may run into sign-up issues before they can try Muse. Common reasons include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Availability restrictions:</strong> The page may say the service is not yet available and invite you to join the waitlist. Creating a Meta account does not automatically grant access to Muse.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>An unstable network or browser setup:</strong> Frequently changing IPs or devices, or enabling multiple proxy extensions at once, can cause failed redirects, lost sessions, and repeated verification requests. Keep your network and browser setup consistent.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Age verification failure:</strong> Some users must confirm their age with a bank card or another option shown on the page. Verification may fail because of card compatibility, billing details, or account information.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
