<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>I</strong><strong>. What Is Meta Muse AI?</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Muse is a personal AI agent from Meta, built on the company’s Muse Spark model. Unlike a traditional chatbot, Muse can understand and answer questions, plan a series of actions around a goal, and use its browser and connected apps to carry out tasks.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Key features of Muse include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Multi-step task execution: Breaks a goal into smaller tasks and works through them step by step.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Browser automation: Uses a dedicated browser to visit websites, fill out forms, and look up information.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Third-party app connections: Can connect to apps such as email, calendars, and Instagram.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Background task execution: Can continue working on certain tasks even after you leave the app.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Content and file handling: Can generate images, create documents, and organize information.</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Muse also runs in its own Muse Secure VM (virtual machine), which hosts the AI agent and browser. Permission controls and approval steps are designed to help protect user data.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In short, traditional AI tools mainly help you find information. Muse goes a step further by helping you get things done.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>II</strong><strong>. What Can Muse AI Do? 8 Practical Use Cases</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Muse is not limited to personal tasks. It can also support content operations, international marketing, cross-border eCommerce, and everyday business workflows.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Content Planning and Creation</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For content creators and marketers, ideas, copy, and creative assets are often scattered across different places, making them time-consuming to organize. Muse can develop topic ideas, draft copy and content outlines, turn scattered materials into organized documents, and use a calendar to help plan publishing schedules. Human review is still important: refine the copy to match your brand voice and avoid generic, repetitive content.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. Social Media Marketing</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Social media teams often have ideas saved in different places, while content calendars and engagement follow-ups are still managed manually. Muse can organize saved posts on Instagram, identify topics that interest your target audience, build a content calendar, and compile interactions that need a response. For actions such as publishing posts or sending messages, review each step and follow the platform permissions and Muse approval prompts.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. International Lead Generation and Outreach</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Finding prospects, researching companies, and writing outreach emails are some of the most time-consuming tasks for cross-border businesses and B2B teams. Muse can research potential customers by industry and target market, organize publicly available company and product information, draft personalized business emails based on prospect details, and outline follow-up plans. Before contacting anyone, verify the information manually and comply with local marketing and privacy laws.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>4. Cross-Border eCommerce and Product Research</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Comparing prices across platforms, checking product specifications, and reading reviews can take a lot of time. Muse can search for products across different platforms, compare prices, specifications, and features, summarize competitor offerings and customer reviews, filter options by budget, and help look up order information. Meta says that in Singapore, Muse has integrations and use cases involving local services such as Shopee, Lazada, Grab, and Agoda, supporting activities like product comparisons, shopping, and travel planning. Check local Meta announcements for the integrations and features currently available in your region.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>5. Advertising and Campaign Planning</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Marketing teams often spend considerable time pulling together data from different channels and preparing reports. Muse can consolidate channel performance data, organize ad creative and copy ideas, review publicly available competitor marketing content, build promotional calendars, and draft reports and optimization recommendations. Actual ad launches and budget changes should still be based on verified data in the relevant ad platforms and reviewed by a person.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>6. Customer Support and Email Management</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sorting and responding to a large volume of customer emails and inquiries every day can be time-consuming. Muse can categorize inbox messages, identify the main questions in customer inquiries, organize order and after-sales information, draft responses to frequently asked questions, compile to-dos, and set reminders. When connecting apps such as Gmail, configure read and send permissions according to your needs; consider starting with read-only access.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>7. Everyday Office Work and Task Management</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Recurring tasks such as meetings, scheduling, and to-do lists can take up more time than expected. Muse can organize meeting schedules, prepare agendas, summarize important points from emails, create work documents, and build task plans for individuals or teams. Business users should first confirm that giving Muse access to work email, calendars, and business materials complies with company policies.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>8. Market Research and Information Gathering</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Keeping up with international markets can be challenging because information is scattered and changes quickly. Muse can search industry news, gather publicly available competitor information, compare product features across brands, organize product information from different markets, and generate industry research summaries. AI-generated summaries may contain omissions or errors, so always verify important data against the original sources.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>III</strong><strong>. How to Get More Out of Muse AI</strong><strong></strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>To make the most of Muse, it helps to understand not only what it can do but also how to give it clear instructions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>1. Be Specific About the Task</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Rather than simply saying, “Plan a trip for me,” include details such as dates, budget, destination, and preferences. Here are a few prompt templates you can adapt:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Customer research: “Research 10 small and medium-sized businesses in the [industry] sector in [target market]. Summarize each company’s background, main products, and publicly listed contact details. Do not send any emails.”</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Content calendar: “Use my saved content ideas and next month’s calendar to create a social media publishing plan with three posts per week. Include a topic, copy outline, and suggested publishing time for each post.”</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Email organization: “Sort customer inquiries from my inbox over the past week into three categories: orders, after-sales support, and pricing requests. List the main questions and items that need a reply in each category. Draft responses only; do not send them.”</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>2. Break Complex Tasks into Smaller Steps</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Ask Muse to research and organize information first. Review the results before moving on to drafting, submitting, or other follow-up actions. This gives you more control than assigning a large task all at once.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>3. Connect Third-Party Apps Thoughtfully</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Muse can connect to email, calendars, and other apps. Before using a connection, check that the app is supported and grant only the permissions you actually need. Avoid giving access to data that is not necessary for the task.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>4. Review the Results</strong><strong></strong></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Manually verify important information such as prices, orders, customer records, and business emails. For sensitive actions such as purchases, sending emails, or submitting forms, review the details carefully and provide confirmation when required.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
